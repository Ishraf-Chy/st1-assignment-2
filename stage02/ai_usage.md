# AI Usage - Stage 2

## AI Requirements

I used AI to review the SmartCare requirements and identify possible problems such as unclear requirements, missing details, assumptions and requirements that may be difficult to test.

The AI review suggested checking areas such as patient information fields, appointment status values, duplicate appointment rules and whether additional features were actually supported by the client requirements.

## Verification

I checked the AI suggestions against the SmartCare case study and the requirements I had already identified.
Suggestions that were supported by the client information were accepted or modified where needed. Suggestions without enough evidence were kept as unverified or rejected.


## Reflection

I used AI to help review the clarity of my reflection and organise my explanation of what I learned from the requirements review process.
